# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming, practical place to collaborate.
Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, and patient.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Respect differing experience levels and deployment environments.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, configuration, and behavior.
- Share enough detail to help others reproduce issues safely.
- Keep examples and logs sanitized.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Sharing credentials, private URLs, or private tracker information.
- Encouraging copyright infringement or other unlawful use.
- Repeatedly derailing issues or pull requests.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments, issues, pull requests, or other
contributions that violate this code of conduct. Maintainers may also block
participants who repeatedly disregard it.
